Urgent Fix – Recovery Must Start Immediately After the First Loss (No Martingale Before Recovery)

The previous fix successfully connected DIFFER_SEQUENCE to the shared recovery engine, but runtime testing has revealed another issue.

Observed behavior

After the first losing trade, the bot does not immediately enter recovery.

Instead, it first executes what appears to be the next strategy trade (effectively behaving like a martingale/progression), and only afterward does it begin recovery.

This is incorrect.

The recovery trade itself is also not immediately switching to the expected recovery contract (OVER 5 or UNDER 4).

The attached runtime results demonstrate this behavior.

Expected behavior

For every AI Bot, the sequence must be:

Execute the normal strategy.
If the trade wins:
Continue the normal strategy.
If the trade loses:
Stop the normal strategy immediately.
Do not execute another normal trade.
Do not advance the sequence.
Do not apply any martingale or progression within the strategy.
The very next trade must be the shared recovery trade:
OVER 5 when appropriate.
UNDER 4 when appropriate.
Only after recovery succeeds should the bot resume its original strategy.

There should never be an intermediate trade between the loss and the recovery trade.

Apply this consistently to all AI Bot strategies

Verify and enforce this behavior for:

OVER 1
UNDER 8
DIFFER (manual)
DIFFER Auto Sequence

Every strategy should:

Loss → Immediate Recovery → Resume Strategy

with no extra strategy trade in between.

strategy loop not checking inRecovery before placing another trade
