Final Transactions Panel Audit

The migration to the new Trading API is functionally complete. Trading, proposals, purchases, and settlements are working correctly. The remaining issues are limited to the Transactions UI.

Please complete the Transactions panel so it reflects the live contract data instead of remaining blank.

1. Entry / Exit Spot

The Entry/Exit Spot column is still blank.

Trace the data flow from:

proposal_open_contract
        ↓
OpenContract.js
        ↓
normalizeContractSpots()
        ↓
transactions-store
        ↓
Transactions table

Verify that the normalized fields are actually being written into the transaction model.

Confirm the UI receives values for:

entry_tick / entry_spot
exit_tick / exit_spot
current_spot
sell_spot

Do not rely on placeholder values. Display the actual contract entry and exit prices from the normalized contract.

2. Total Stake

The summary currently shows:

Total Stake: 0.00 USD

even though multiple trades have executed.

Audit how the summary is calculated.

It should equal:

Σ buy_price (stake) of all completed trades

or whatever transaction collection the panel is displaying.

Do not hardcode or cache values.

The summary should always recalculate from the Transactions store whenever a trade is added, updated, or removed.

3. Total Payout

The summary also shows:

Total Payout: 0.00 USD

It should equal:

Σ payout of all settled winning trades

or the equivalent normalized payout field from the new Trading API.

Verify that the payout field is correctly normalized before aggregation.

4. Total Profit/Loss

Currently:

Total Profit/Loss: 0.00 USD

despite multiple winning trades.

Compute it directly from the transaction model:

Σ profit

or

Σ (sell_price - buy_price)

depending on how the normalized contract is represented.

The summary must update immediately after each contract settles.

5. Single Source of Truth

Do not calculate these totals inside the UI component.

Instead:

proposal_open_contract
        ↓
normalizeContractSpots()
        ↓
transactions-store
        ↓
derived summary selectors

Total Stake
Total Payout
Total Profit/Loss

The Transactions component should simply render the derived values.

Please verify with one completed trading session that:

✅ Entry Spot displays correctly.
✅ Exit Spot displays correctly.
✅ Buy Price displays correctly.
✅ Sell Price/Payout displays correctly.
✅ Total Stake increments after every purchase.
