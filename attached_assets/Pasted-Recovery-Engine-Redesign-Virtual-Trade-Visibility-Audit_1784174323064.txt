Recovery Engine Redesign + Virtual Trade Visibility Audit

Before making changes, audit the existing recovery flow and then implement the behavior described below.

Current Problem

The current AI Bot recovery behavior is not what I want.

Right now, after a loss:

Martingale increases the stake.
But the bot continues using the original contract type/strategy.
Recovery is therefore tied to the original strategy.

This is incorrect.

Desired Recovery Behavior

Recovery should become a completely separate mode.

When any AI Bot strategy loses:

Detect the loss.
Immediately suspend the original strategy.
Enter Recovery Mode.
Recovery Mode must trade ONLY:
OVER 5
or UNDER 4

according to the existing recovery-selection rules.

The original strategy should not place any trades while recovery is active.

Example

Assume:

Initial strategy = DIFFER
Stake = 1
Martingale multiplier = 2

Trade 1:

DIFFER
Stake = 1
Result = LOSS

Immediately:

Recovery Trade 1:

OVER 5 or UNDER 4
Stake = 2

If LOSS:

Recovery Trade 2:

OVER 5 or UNDER 4
Stake = 4

If LOSS:

Recovery Trade 3:

OVER 5 or UNDER 4
Stake = 8

Continue recovery progression until a WIN occurs.

Only after a recovery WIN:

Reset recovery state.
Return to the original strategy.
Resume normal trading.
Apply To All AI Bots

This recovery behavior must be identical for:

OVER 1
UNDER 8
DIFFER (Manual)
DIFFER Auto Sequence
MATCHES
Any other AI Bot strategy using the shared recovery engine

There should be one shared Recovery Engine.

The only thing that differs between bots should be the entry strategy.

Recovery Rules

During recovery:

Do not execute the original strategy.
Do not alternate between recovery and strategy trades.
Do not place any normal strategy trade until recovery is complete.
Recovery trades must exclusively use:
OVER 5
UNDER 4

until a recovery win occurs.

VIRTUAL

what the bot decided

when recovery started

when recovery finished

in real time.
